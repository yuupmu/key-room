# Salamander Grand Piano samples

- Creator: Alexander Holm
- Instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano
- Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- Original project: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

This app includes 30 MP3 samples from the Tone.js Salamander set, selected at spaced note positions. The audio files were not edited; Tone.Sampler changes playback pitch for notes between samples. Other velocity layers and release samples from the full instrument are not included.
